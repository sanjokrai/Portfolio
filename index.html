#About

I'm Sanjok, a student working toward a career in ethical hacking and offensive security. Most of what I know came from taking things apart in a home lab — spinning up vulnerable VMs, breaking my own network, and figuring out afterward what actually happened.

I spend a lot of time in CTFs and on platforms built around real-world attack paths, because reading about a vulnerability and finding it yourself are two very different skills. Right now I'm working toward a foundational security certification and building out a small collection of writeups and tools as I go.

Outside of security work, I like documenting what I learn clearly enough that a beginner could follow it — this site is part of that habit.
